Meet Your Dentist

Dr. Hemant Seth, DDS

Dr. Seth brings over 16 years of clinical experience spanning general dentistry, oral surgery, and implantology. After earning his Bachelor of Dental Surgery in 2009, he went on to complete advanced training in implantology, including a diploma from ADC and a mini-residency through DACE Bengaluru in partnership with the University at Buffalo, NY (Zimmer Biomet).

Before coming to Canada, Dr. Seth ran a successful private practice and served as Zonal Operational Manager and Senior Consultant overseeing multiple corporate clinics with Clove Dental in India. He was recognized as a Rising Star in Implantology in 2020 and has published numerous clinical case studies in the field.

Now Canadian DDS-certified and practising in the GTA, Dr. Seth is skilled in oral surgery, complex endodontic treatments, periodontal surgeries, bone grafting, and comprehensive general dentistry.

Dental implants · Brampton

Dental implants that look and feel like your own teeth

A dental implant replaces the root of a missing tooth with a small titanium post, topped with a custom crown. It is the closest thing to a natural tooth, and with good care it can last for decades.

The process

What to expect

  1. ConsultationWe examine your mouth, take imaging and walk you through your options and costs.
  2. Implant placementA titanium post is placed under local anaesthetic, with bone grafting if needed.
  3. HealingOver a few months the implant fuses with your jawbone.
  4. Final crownA custom crown or bridge is attached and colour-matched to your teeth.

Questions

Dental implant FAQ

How much do dental implants cost in Brampton?

The cost depends on how many teeth are being replaced, your bone health, whether you need bone grafting and the type of final restoration. After your consultation we give you a full written estimate.

Does getting an implant hurt?

Implants are placed under local anaesthetic, so you should feel pressure but not pain. Most patients manage any soreness afterwards with over-the-counter pain relief.

How long does the implant process take?

After placement, the implant usually needs a few months to fuse with the jawbone before the final crown is attached. We will give you a timeline at your consultation.

What if I don't have enough bone for an implant?

Dr. Seth performs bone grafting, which rebuilds the jawbone so an implant has a strong foundation.

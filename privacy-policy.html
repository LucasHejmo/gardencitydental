Privacy policy

Garden Square Family Dentistry respects your privacy and protects your personal health information in line with Ontario's Personal Health Information Protection Act (PHIPA).

What we collect

To provide dental care, we collect information such as your name, contact details, date of birth, medical and dental history, insurance or CDCP details, and treatment records. When you use our website form, we collect the details you choose to send us, such as your name, phone number, email and reason for visit.

How we use it

We use your information to provide and plan your care, book and remind you of appointments, bill your insurer or government plan, and meet our legal and regulatory obligations. We do not sell your information.

Who we share it with

We share information only as needed for your care or as required by law, for example with your insurer or CDCP to process claims, with specialists or labs involved in your treatment, or with our regulator if required.

Our website

Appointment requests sent through our website are delivered to our clinic by email through a secure form service. Please do not include detailed medical information in the website form. Our website does not use advertising cookies.

How we protect it

We keep records secure using physical, technical and administrative safeguards, and only staff who need your information to do their jobs can access it.

Your rights

You may ask to see or correct your records, or ask questions about how we handle your information. Contact us at info@gardensquarefamilydentistry.com or (365) 230-9200. You also have the right to contact the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Ontario.

Accessibility

We are committed to providing accessible care and services in line with the Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act (AODA). If you need an accommodation for your visit, or have trouble using this website, please call us at (365) 230-9200 and we will be happy to help.

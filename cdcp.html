Questions

CDCP questions

Do you accept the Canadian Dental Care Plan (CDCP)?

Yes. Garden Square Family Dentistry accepts CDCP patients and bills the plan directly.

Who is eligible for CDCP?

You may qualify if you do not have access to private dental insurance, your adjusted family net income is under $90,000, you (and your spouse or partner) have filed your taxes, and you are a resident of Canada for tax purposes.

How do I apply for CDCP?

Apply through the Government of Canada at Canada.ca or by calling Service Canada at 1-833-537-4342. Once you are approved, you will receive a member card to bring to your appointment.

Will I have to pay anything?

Depending on your family income, you may have a co-payment, and some services may not be fully covered. We will review your coverage and give you a clear estimate before any treatment.

Is orthodontic treatment covered by CDCP?

Orthodontic treatment is not currently covered by CDCP. We are happy to go over other payment options for braces or clear aligners.

Dental emergency FAQ

Do you offer same-day emergency appointments?

Yes. Call us at (365) 230-9200 and we will do our best to see you the same day.

What counts as a dental emergency?

Severe toothache, a broken, cracked or knocked-out tooth, a lost filling or crown, swelling, an abscess, or bleeding that won't stop are all reasons to call us right away.

What should I do if a tooth is knocked out?

Pick it up by the crown, not the root. Rinse it gently without scrubbing, try to place it back in the socket or keep it in milk, and call us immediately. Getting seen within an hour gives the best chance of saving the tooth.

When should I go to the hospital instead?

If you have swelling that affects your breathing or swallowing, a high fever with facial swelling, or an injury to your jaw, go to the nearest emergency room.

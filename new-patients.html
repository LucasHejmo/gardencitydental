Questions

Frequently asked

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes. We welcome new patients of all ages. Register early and you'll get priority scheduling.

Do you bill insurance directly?

Yes. We offer direct insurance billing, so you only pay the portion your plan doesn't cover. Call us to confirm your specific plan.

How much do dental implants cost?

Cost depends on how many teeth are being replaced, your bone health and the final restoration. After your consultation we give you a full written estimate.

What should I do in a dental emergency?

Call us right away at (365) 230-9200. We offer same-day appointments for toothaches, trauma and urgent needs. If you have swelling that affects breathing or swallowing, go to the nearest emergency room.

At what age should my child first see a dentist?

We recommend a first visit by age one, or within six months of the first tooth appearing.

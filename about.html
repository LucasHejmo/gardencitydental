Meet your dentist

Dr. Hemant SethDDS (Canadian Equivalency)

Dr. Seth has more than 16 years of experience across general dentistry, oral surgery and implantology. Before coming to the GTA, he ran his own private practice and served as Zonal Operational Manager and Senior Consultant for multiple corporate clinics with Clove Dental in India.

He has published several clinical case studies and was recognized as a Rising Star in Implantology in 2020. Today he brings that depth of experience to families in Brampton.
